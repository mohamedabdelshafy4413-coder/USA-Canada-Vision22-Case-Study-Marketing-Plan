# Vision22 B2B Digital Growth Case Study

## Company Positioning
Vision22 is positioned as an integrated B2B digital growth partner helping companies in the USA and Canada generate qualified opportunities and build predictable sales pipelines.

## Market Challenge
Many B2B companies have strong products and services but struggle with:
- Limited digital visibility
- Weak lead generation systems
- Poor conversion paths
- Lack of consistent content and authority building

## Growth Opportunity
Vision22 provides a complete growth framework combining:
- Market research
- ICP development
- Lead generation
- SEO
- Paid acquisition
- Website conversion optimization
- Content and authority building

## Target Industries
- Manufacturing
- Construction & Engineering
- B2B SaaS
- Distribution
- Professional Services

## 90-Day Execution Plan

### Month 1
- Market research
- Account targeting
- Campaign setup
- Digital audits

### Month 2
- Launch outreach campaigns
- Generate qualified conversations
- Optimize acquisition channels

### Month 3
- Scale winning campaigns
- Convert opportunities into retainers
- Build long-term growth partnerships

## Expected Business Model
Monthly retainers from $7,000 to $50,000 depending on scope and company size.
