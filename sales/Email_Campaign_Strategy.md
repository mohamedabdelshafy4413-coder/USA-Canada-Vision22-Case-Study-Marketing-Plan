# Vision22 B2B Email Campaign Strategy

## Objective
Generate qualified B2B opportunities in the USA and Canada.

## Target Accounts
- Manufacturing companies
- Construction companies
- Engineering firms
- B2B SaaS companies
- Distribution companies

## Campaign Structure

### Email 1 - Introduction
Subject: Growth Opportunities For [Company Name]

Focus:
Identify digital growth opportunities and request a short conversation.

### Email 2 - Value Follow-up
Share potential improvements:
- Lead generation
- SEO visibility
- Website conversion
- Market expansion

### Email 3 - Case Study
Provide relevant industry examples and outcomes.

### Email 4 - Final Follow-up
Offer a short strategy discussion.

## KPIs
- Open Rate
- Reply Rate
- Meeting Rate
- Proposal Rate
- Closed Deals

## Goal
Build a predictable B2B sales pipeline through targeted outreach.
